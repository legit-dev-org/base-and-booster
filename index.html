Step 1 · Structure

The 4 Structural Bases

Bases are unspiced. They decide the body of the dish: light, deep, bright, or creamy.

1 · Clean Mirepoix

Sweet, un-browned onion, carrot & celery. A light, invisible backbone.

2 · Allium-Tomato Jam

Caramelized onion, roasted garlic & tomato paste. Deep umami comfort.

3 · Green Citrus Slurry

Charred poblano, herbs, ginger & citrus. Bright, acidic, alive.

4 · Velvet Garlic Cream

Cashew-garlic emulsion. Turns any sauce silky, dairy-free.

Step 2 · Identity

The 4 Flavor Boosters

Boosters are hyper-concentrated spice and aromatic fats. They decide where in the world the dish is from.

A · Euro-Bistro

Red wine, cracked pepper, thyme, rosemary & Dijon.

B · East & SE Asian

Ginger, scallion, lemongrass, galangal & sesame.

C · South Asian Masala

Mustard seed, cumin & curry leaf bloomed in hot fat.

D · Latin Adobo

Ancho, smoky chipotle, Mexican oregano & cumin.

Step 3 · Stack & Cook
